## How this was measured

`analyze.py` recomputes the moving averages, the swing structure, the open fair value gaps, the order block, and the discount/premium location from `data/gc_daily.json`, `data/gc_hourly.json`, and `data/gc_weekly.json`. Those files are the Yahoo Finance `GC=F` snapshot used for this read. `python3 analyze.py --live` refreshes them.

Swing points use a 2-bar fractal. A fair value gap is the untraded space between candle 1 and candle 3; it stays open until price fully crosses it. The order block is the last up-close daily candle before the bar that broke the prior swing low. The optimal trade entry is the 0.62–0.79 retracement of the active dealing range, measured from the swing low.
